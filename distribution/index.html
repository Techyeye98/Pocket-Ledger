The problem

Your money already has a job to do — it shouldn't owe your attention to an ad engine.

Most finance apps fund free features by studying your habits and selling that shadow. Pocket Ledger treats your ledger like a private journal: it commits on your device, and the only copy that travels is the one you send, to a sheet you own.

The old way

Your data, multi-tenant

Transaction history parked on shared cloud databases, mined for telemetry, monetized by recommendation rails.

The Pocket Ledger way

Your data, your perimeter

A Room database on your device is the source of truth. Google Sheets is an explicit mirror — synced only in the foreground, only when you trigger it.

Two soil types, one rule: nothing leaves your hand until you open it.

The system

Sovereign by design.

Get started

Three doors. One ledger.

Android app, iPhone Shortcut, or straight into Sheets — pick your way in. Setup takes minutes.

v2.0 apk

Android app

Native app, direct sideload — no Play Services required, no app store account.

  1. Download and install the APK, enabling sideload.
  2. Make your V2 Sheet copy and deploy the Apps Script as a Web App.
  3. Paste your Web App URL into Settings → Google Sheets Sync, test, log.
Download APK checking…

shortcut

Apple Shortcut

Not a native app — a Shortcut workflow you install, then configure with your Web App URL.

  1. Get the Shortcut, install it into Apple Shortcuts.
  2. Make your V1 Sheet copy and paste in your Web App URL.
  3. Say “Hey Siri, log expense” or map it to the Action Button.
Get iPhone Shortcut
template

Google Sheets

Copy the template into your own Drive. That copy is your database — we never host it.

  1. Make a private copy of the master template.
  2. Open Extensions → Apps Script and deploy as a Web App.
  3. Connect either the Android app or the Shortcut to that endpoint.
